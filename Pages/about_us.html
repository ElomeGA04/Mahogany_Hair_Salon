About Mahogany Hair Salon

Our Founder

Who we are

At Mahogany Hair Salon, we are dedicated to providing exceptional hair care services in a welcoming and stylish environment. Our team of experienced stylists are passionate about helping you look and feel your best. Whether you're looking for a trendy haircut, vibrant color, or a relaxing treatment, we have you covered. We use high-quality products and the latest techniques to ensure you leave our salon with a smile on your face. Visit us today and experience the difference at Mahogany Hair Salon!


Our History

What started as a small dorm-room service in 2022, washing and braiding friends' hair, quickly grew. Mahogany; our founder, noticed a gap for affordable, trustworthy hair care near campus. Today, Mahogany Hair Salon is a beloved storefront and quality meet.

Our Mission

Our mission at Mahogany Hair Salon is to provide high-quality, affordable beauty services and products in a welcoming space, with a special focus on students and young professionals far from home.

Our Vision

To expand Mahogany into a trusted brand across multiple university towns, empowering student-enterpreneurs and making self-care accessible to all.

Our Target Audience

  • Far from home University Students
  • Clients with natural, curly, and transitioning hair
  • Anyone seeking budget-friendly manicures, pedicures, and makeup serices
  • Gift buyers
  • Residents
